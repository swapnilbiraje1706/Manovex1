Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 3 June 2026

This policy explains how cancellation works for Manovex Pro annual access.

1. Current Plan Type

The current Manovex Pro plan is a prepaid annual digital access plan. Unless the checkout page clearly states otherwise, it does not auto-renew and does not require a recurring cancellation request.

2. Access After Cancellation

If a future recurring plan is offered and you cancel it, paid access will normally remain available until the end of the already-paid billing period, unless the account is terminated for violation of our Terms and Conditions.

3. How To Request Cancellation

If your account has an active recurring arrangement or you need help stopping future billing, email info@manovex.in with your registered email address and payment details.

4. Refunds After Cancellation

Cancellation does not automatically create a refund. Refund requests are reviewed under the Refund Policy.

5. Account Deletion

You may request account deletion by contacting support. Some payment, tax, fraud prevention, and legal records may be retained where required by law or payment compliance obligations.
