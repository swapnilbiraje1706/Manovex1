Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Manovex Ventures Private Limited collects, uses, and protects information when you use Manovex at https://manovex.in.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect account information such as your name, email address, authentication provider details, subscription status, reading progress, saved document metadata, session history, support messages, and payment status.

2. Document Privacy

Manovex is designed so uploaded files are processed locally in your browser where possible. We do not intentionally upload your full PDF, EPUB, or text files to our servers for reading extraction. Some saved reading progress, titles, metadata, and user settings may be stored so you can resume reading.

3. Payments

Payments are processed by Razorpay and supported payment partners. We receive payment confirmation, order identifiers, subscription status, and limited transaction metadata. We do not store full card numbers, CVV, or sensitive payment instrument details.

4. Authentication

When you sign in by email or Google, authentication is handled through Supabase and the selected identity provider. We may receive your email address, name, profile identifier, and login status to operate your account.

5. Local Storage

Manovex may use browser local storage or similar browser storage to save settings, local reading state, documents, progress, and interface preferences on your device.

6. How We Use Information

We use information to provide the service, manage accounts and subscriptions, unlock paid features, save progress, improve product reliability, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, and comply with legal or payment obligations.

7. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share necessary data with service providers such as Supabase, Razorpay, hosting providers, analytics or security tools, and legal authorities where required by law.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should keep their account credentials secure.

9. Data Requests

You may request account support, correction, or deletion by contacting info@manovex.in. Some records may be retained where required for legal, tax, fraud prevention, or payment compliance purposes.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at info@manovex.in.
